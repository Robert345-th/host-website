Privacy policy

Last updated 4 September 2026

ZedEvents helps people in Zambia find and book event services. This page explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Your name, phone, email, city, and date of birth when you create an account.
  • Shop details you enter: business name, bio, photos, and addresses.
  • Services you post, messages you send, reviews, and reports.
  • Approximate location if you allow it, so we can show nearby services.

How we use it

We use this information to run the app: show your shop, send messages, approve vendors, and keep the marketplace safe. We do not sell your phone number to advertisers.

Who can see it

Public service posts and shop profiles are visible to anyone on ZedEvents. Your phone is shown to people you message, not on the public feed.

Contact

If you want your account deleted, use Settings → Delete my account, or contact support from the number on your shop registration.
